### Selected Projects

**PREPARE — Multimodal Assessment & Intelligent Learning Platform**  
A medical training platform that brings assessment data together across learners, instructors, and training environments. I integrated audio, video, scores, virtual patient, and EHR data; built services and data pipelines; and advanced LLM, RAG, NLP, and event-detection workflows. I also helped automate deployments across six partner sites.

[Learn about PREPARE at The University of Toledo](https://www.utoledo.edu/med/research/prepare/)
